# Angiography SLR: screening and extraction data

Data and code that accompany the systematic review *A Systematic Literature Review on Methods for Angiographic Sequence Analysis*.

## screening/

- `stage1_llm_decisions.csv`: all 5,064 unique records screened at title/abstract level. `llm_decision` is the stage-1 LLM screening decision (134 include). `final_decision` is the decision used in the review (122 include): for the 491 records in the validation sample it is the consensus of two human reviewers, and for the remaining 4,573 pipeline-excluded records it is the pipeline decision.
- `screening_double_annotation.csv`: the 491-record validation sample (all 134 pipeline includes plus 357 random pipeline excludes) with the pipeline decision, the two independent human decisions and the consensus label.
- `pipeline/`: the search and screening code. `02_slr_paper_fetcher.py` queries PubMed and Semantic Scholar (set `ENTREZ_EMAIL`, `NCBI_API_KEY`, `S2_API_KEY`); `03_stage1_screening.py` holds the inclusion and exclusion gate prompts; `03b_stage1_prefiltered.py` runs the final screen (inclusion gate on all records, exclusion gate on survivors plus PubMed non-research publication types), with `SCREENING_DESIGN.md` describing the design. Screening used `qwen3.8:27b` through Ollama at temperature 0.
- `screening_agreement.py`: reproduces Cohen's kappa between the two reviewers (0.978, 95% CI 0.957-0.999) and between the pipeline and the consensus, and writes the appendix table.

## extraction/

- `INSTRUCTIONS.md`, `schema.json` (generated by `make_schema.py`), `validate.py`: the per-paper instructions, JSON schema and validator used for full-text extraction with Claude Opus 5.5, one fresh model context per paper.
- `out/R####.json`: the 115 validated extraction records (one per included study). Full-text exclusions and merges are in `extraction/out_excluded/`: R1725 and R2430 are meeting abstracts excluded under E1 at full text; R4154, R2434 and R4671 are earlier versions (preprint or conference paper) of the included journal articles R3555, R1238 and R4479 and are counted once. R4482 was reclassified by the reviewers from `none` to `frame_aggregation` (temporal pooling of frames). Every `quote` field is a verbatim excerpt of at most 40 words from the article.
- `manifest.csv`: record id, title, year, venue and DOI for the 122 eligible studies; the 115 included studies are those with a record in `out/`, and R4468 and R4475 (full text not retrievable) have none.
- `v2_to_legacy.py`: converts the records to the flat format used by the figure scripts.

Full texts are not redistributed because of copyright, so to run `validate.py` you need to supply the article text as `extraction/fulltext/R####.md` yourself.

## analysis/

Scripts that produce the review's figures and tables from `extraction/out/`. From the repo root run `bash analysis/run_all.sh` (needs numpy, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn, scipy; `extraction/validate.py` also needs jsonschema). It converts the records with `v2_to_legacy.py` into `results/`, writes the figures and tables to `analysis-results/`, and prints the CLAIM 2024 cohort comparison (two-sided Fisher's exact test per cluster, Holm-adjusted) from `claim_fisher.py`.
